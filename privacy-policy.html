PageHub Agency – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

1. Who we are

PageHub Agency is a Windows desktop application that helps marketing agencies publish and schedule content on Facebook Pages they own or are authorized to manage, using the official Meta Graph API. It is provided by Rose Travel Service Co., Ltd. (contact: dxvantinh@gmail.com).

2. What data the app accesses

After you log in with Facebook and choose which Pages to grant, the app accesses only what you approved:

  • Your Facebook user ID and name (to show which account is connected).
  • The list of Pages and Business Managers you manage, and Page access tokens issued by Meta.
  • Basic Page information (name, category, follower count, website, phone) and the Page's own posts with their reaction, comment, share and view counts.
  • Content you create in the app (post text, images, videos, first comments, schedules).

The app never asks for or stores your Facebook password and does not use browser cookies.

3. How the data is used

  • To publish, schedule and delete posts on the Pages you selected, and to post the first comment you wrote.
  • To show Page and post statistics and export reports for you or your clients.
  • To update Page information (about, website, phone, profile picture) when you request it.
  • To let Business Manager admins assign Pages to their own team members.

We do not use the data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than the features you use.

4. Where the data is stored

  • All data stays on your computer, in %AppData%\PageHubAgency, encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI) so only your Windows user can read it.
  • We do not operate a server that receives your Facebook data. The app talks directly to Meta's Graph API.
  • The app only contacts our update server to check for new versions; no Facebook data is sent.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties. Data leaves your computer only when the app sends your requests to Meta (Facebook).

6. Retention and deletion

Data is kept on your computer until you delete it. See our Data Deletion Instructions. You can also remove the app's access at any time in Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations.

7. Security

Tokens and data are encrypted at rest with Windows DPAPI. Connections to Meta use HTTPS. Software updates are digitally signed and verified before installation.

8. Children

The app is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 13.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Questions: dxvantinh@gmail.com.
